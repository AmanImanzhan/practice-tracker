# Privacy Policy — Practice Tracker

**Effective date:** October 2, 2026

Practice Tracker for Musicians ("Practice Tracker", "the App") is developed by Amangali Imanzhan, an independent developer based in Georgia ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information Practice Tracker collects, why, and how you can control it.

## Information We Collect

When you create an account and use Practice Tracker, we collect:

- **Account information**: your email address and name, and (if you sign in with Apple or Google) the name and email those services share with us.
- **Practice data you enter**: dates and durations of your practice sessions, repertoire/pieces you log, and any notes you write about a session.
- **App preferences**: your chosen theme, practice goal, instrument category, and notification preferences.
- **Purchase information** (future feature): Practice Tracker does not currently offer any in-app purchase. If a paid feature is introduced in a future update, we will store the Apple transaction ID and purchase date to verify that purchase and let you restore it later. We do not see or store your payment card details — Apple handles payment directly.

We do not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, or any tracking data. Practice Tracker does not show ads and does not use any analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information only to:

- Provide the core functionality of the app (storing and displaying your practice history)
- If a paid feature is introduced in the future, verify that purchase with Apple and let you restore it on a new device or account
- Let you sign in and keep your account secure
- Respond to support requests you send us

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with advertisers.

## Who Processes Your Data

- **Supabase** (database, authentication, and backend functions) — hosted in the EU (eu-west-1 / Ireland). All the data listed above is stored here.
- **Apple** — if you sign in with "Sign in with Apple", or when you make a purchase through the App Store, Apple processes the relevant authentication and payment information under its own privacy policy.
- **Google** — if you sign in with Google, Google processes the relevant authentication information under its own privacy policy.

We do not use any other third-party service to process your personal data.

Our database is located in the EU, but Apple and Google may process data outside the EU under their own privacy policies.

## Data Retention and Deletion

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from within the app: **Settings → Account Profile → Delete Account**. This immediately and permanently deletes your profile, practice sessions, repertoire, notes, and purchase records from our database. If you signed in with Apple, deleting your account also revokes the app's access via your Apple ID. This action cannot be undone.

Copies of your data may also exist in our database backups for a limited time after deletion. These backups are retained only for disaster-recovery purposes and are automatically deleted on a rolling schedule — we do not restore an individual account from a backup after you've deleted it.

## Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

We process your data to perform our contract with you — that is, to provide the app you've signed up to use (storing your practice history, syncing your account, and so on). Where you make a purchase, we also process the related data to fulfill our obligations to Apple as the payment processor for that purchase.

## Your Rights

If you are located in the European Union/European Economic Area, you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), including the right to:

- Access the personal data we hold about you
- Correct inaccurate data (via Edit Profile in the app)
- Delete your data (via Delete Account in the app, or by contacting us)
- Export your data (via Export Practice Data in the app)
- Object to or restrict how we process your data
- Lodge a complaint with your country's data protection supervisory authority

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email below.

## Children's Privacy

Practice Tracker is not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the "Effective date" above.
